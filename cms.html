Deploy to Vercel

To connect this CMS to your live Vercel site, follow these steps:

1
Upload both index.html and cms.html to your GitHub repository.
2
Go to vercel.com, connect your GitHub repo, and deploy.
3
Your landing page will be at yoursite.vercel.app and CMS at yoursite.vercel.app/cms.html.
Tip: Every time you make changes in this CMS and save, re-upload the updated files to GitHub — Vercel will auto-redeploy your site.
